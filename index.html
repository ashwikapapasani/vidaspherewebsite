Our Microgreens Initiative

What Are Microgreens?

VidaSphere addresses two connected challenges: limited access to fresh, nutritious food in low-income urban Philadelphia communities, and social isolation among older adults in suburban neighborhoods.

How We Distribute Them

Microgreens are nutrient-dense crops that can be grown quickly in small indoor spaces using minimal water and resources, making them an accessible and sustainable source of fresh produce. Research shows they contain high levels of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.

Through VidaSphere, we increase access to microgreens in underserved Philadelphia communities while engaging residents of suburban senior living facilities in growing and distributing them. This model promotes healthier diets, creates meaningful community engagement for older adults, and helps build a more equitable and sustainable local food system.

Our Mission

What started as a small group of students passionate about healthcare has grown into a thriving nonprofit with over 50 members from grades 8 to 11. United by a shared drive to make a difference, we've built a supportive, purpose-driven community of future medical leaders.

Our mission is to support critical healthcare for underserved communities in Philadelphia. In partnership with the Drexel Office of Community Health, we bring essential healthcare services to those who need them most.

If you're a student interested in healthcare, service, or simply being part of something meaningful — we'd love to have you on board.
